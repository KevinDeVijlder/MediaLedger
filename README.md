# ✨ MediaLedger
A personal cataloging app for organizing and tracking your physical and digital media collection

## 🌟 Features

- **Dashboard Overview** – Quick glance of all items and collections.
- **Items Management** – Add, edit, and view your media items.
- **Collections** – Organize items into collections for easy navigation.
- **Tags & Platforms** – Assign platforms, media types, and tags to items.
- **Responsive Sidebar** – Collapsible navigation with intuitive icons.
- **Upload Media** – Attach cover images to collections and items.
